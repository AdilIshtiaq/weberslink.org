How much does it cost?

The Lead System is $490/month plus a one-time $1,490 setup. Growth Engine (adds local SEO, Google Business Profile and ads management) is $990/month plus setup. If you only want a website you own outright, that starts at $1,490 one-time.

Why not just build it myself with AI tools?

You can — and for some businesses that's the right call. Most owners we talk to don't have time to connect a website, chatbot, calendar, CRM, follow-ups and reviews, keep them all working, and improve them every month. That ongoing work is what you're paying us for.

How long does setup take?

Most Lead Systems go live in 2–3 weeks. The biggest factor is how quickly we get your service details, photos and feedback — we'll give you a dated plan up front.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. Plans are month-to-month and you can cancel anytime. If you cancel, you keep your website and domain — and we'll help you move hosting if you want.

You're based in Pakistan — how does working across time zones work?

We schedule calls at times that suit you, whether you're in New York, London or Berlin. Progress lives in a shared board you can check any time, and we reply to messages within 24 hours — usually much faster. Many clients like that work continues while they sleep.

How do payments work?

The setup fee is paid 50% to start and 50% at launch. Monthly plans begin at launch and are billed at the start of each month. We accept bank transfer, Wise or PayPal in USD, EUR or GBP.

Do I own my website?

Yes. The design, code, content and domain are yours. We hand over all logins at launch, and you're never locked in to us.

Will my site be GDPR compliant?

For EU and UK clients we set up a privacy policy page, cookie consent where needed and privacy-friendly analytics, and we keep forms collecting only the data you need.

What exactly is the AI assistant?

A chat assistant on your website (and optionally WhatsApp) trained on your services, prices and FAQs. It answers questions instantly, collects the visitor's details and books calls into your calendar — and hands over to you whenever a human is needed.

Do you only work with contractors?

Contractors and home service businesses are our focus, but the same system works for builders, clinics, accountants, consultants and any business that lives on enquiries and appointments. See industries we serve.
