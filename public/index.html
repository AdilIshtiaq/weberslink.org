FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Ask us on a free call.

How much does a website cost?

Our packages start at $1,490 for a Launch site and $2,990 for Growth (website + AI assistant + automation). After a short call we send a fixed quote, so you know the full price before any work starts.

How long does it take?

Most Launch sites go live in 2–3 weeks and Growth projects in 3–5 weeks. The biggest factor is how quickly content and feedback come back — we'll give you a dated plan up front.

You're based in Pakistan — how does working across time zones work?

We schedule calls at times that suit you, whether you're in New York, London or Berlin. Progress lives in a shared board you can check any time, and we reply to messages within 24 hours — usually much faster. Many clients like that work continues while they sleep.

How do payments work?

Typically 50% to start and 50% at launch, paid by bank transfer, Wise or PayPal in USD, EUR or GBP. Monthly plans are billed at the start of each month.

Do I own my website?

Yes. The design, code, content and domain are yours. We hand over all logins at launch, and you're never locked in to us.

Will my site be GDPR compliant?

For EU and UK clients we set up a privacy policy page, cookie consent where needed and privacy-friendly analytics, and we keep forms collecting only the data you need.

What exactly is the AI assistant?

A chat assistant on your website (and optionally WhatsApp) trained on your services, prices and FAQs. It answers questions instantly, collects the visitor's details and books calls into your calendar — and hands over to you whenever a human is needed.

What happens after launch?

Every project includes a support period for fixes and small changes. After that you can keep the site yourself, or move to a monthly plan for SEO, ads, updates and new automations.
