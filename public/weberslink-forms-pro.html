Weberslink Forms Pro

A complete drag-and-drop form builder for WordPress. Version 1.2.0 · Requires WordPress 5.8+ and PHP 7.4+ · GPL-2.0-or-later

Download weberslink-forms-pro.zip

What you get

  • Builder: drag & drop, 45+ field types, 30+ templates, multi-step forms, conditional logic, calculations, input masks, auto-save.
  • Any theme: inherits your theme's fonts and colours, readable on light and dark backgrounds, RTL ready. Shortcode, Gutenberg block, widget and Elementor widget.
  • Entries: search, filters, notes, edit, reply by email, print/PDF, CSV export, reports and conversion tracking. Quiz scoring and conditional confirmations.
  • Emails: unlimited notifications and auto-responders with merge tags, attachments and conditions.
  • Payments: Stripe Checkout, PayPal or offline, products, quantities, coupons, tax and order summary.
  • Integrations: webhooks (Zapier, Make, n8n, Google Sheets), Slack, Discord, Teams, Telegram, Mailchimp, create WordPress users.
  • Spam & privacy: honeypot, time trap, rate limit, Akismet, reCAPTCHA, hCaptcha, Turnstile; IP anonymising, auto-delete, GDPR export/erase.

Install

  1. In WordPress go to Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin and choose the zip.
  2. Activate, then open Weberslink Forms → Add new and pick a template.
  3. Embed with [weberslink_form id="1"].
