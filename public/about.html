About us

One team that builds and stays.

WebersLink is an in-house tech team for growing businesses. We design, build and look after the automation, AI agents, websites, software and CMS sites our clients run on, working with businesses in the United Kingdom and the United States from our engineering base in Lahore, Pakistan.

Who we are

WebersLink started with a simple observation: most small and growing businesses don't need a big agency or a room full of freelancers. They need one dependable team that understands how their business works, builds the right tools, and is still there when something needs changing next month.

That is what we are. The same engineers who plan your project build it, connect it to the tools you already use, and keep improving it afterwards. You always know who to ask, and nothing gets lost in a hand-off.

What we do

We focus on five things, and we explain each of them in plain words:

  • Automation: software that does repetitive work for you, such as reading emails and forms, updating your CRM, sending follow-ups and building reports.
  • AI voice and chat agents: an AI receptionist that answers calls, website chat and WhatsApp, answers questions and books appointments.
  • In-house tech team: your own engineering team, month to month, without recruiting.
  • Web and software development: fast websites, web apps, portals, dashboards and online stores.
  • CMS design and development: websites your team can update without calling a developer, on WordPress or a headless CMS.

What we've built

We don't only build for clients; we build and run our own products, and we use the same skills on every client project:

  • Outreach Engine: a cold-email platform that sends from several mailboxes with warm-up, stops when someone replies, tracks link clicks and reports everything on one dashboard.
  • Online Booking Platform: booking pages, a staff dashboard, reminders, client records, team logins and Stripe deposits for appointment businesses.
  • WebersLink AI Assistant: the AI chat agent on this website, trained on our services and prices.
  • Free WordPress plugins: forms, SEO, security, email delivery, popups, cookie consent, image optimisation and redirects, free to download.

Alongside our own products, we have shipped more than 50 websites for builders, home-service companies, retailers, manufacturers and agencies.

How we work

  • Plain English. No jargon. If we can't explain it simply, we haven't understood it yet.
  • Fixed prices. You get one clear price before we start, and monthly plans are month to month.
