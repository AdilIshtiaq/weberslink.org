FAQ

Common questions.

What happens when it isn't sure?

Anything unclear goes to a review queue for a person to check, instead of being filed silently.

Which tools does it work with?

We use reliable tools such as Zapier, Make, n8n, your CRM's native workflows and custom code via APIs where needed, and pick the simplest option that does the job. You own every account and every automation.

Which CRM should I use?

If you don't have one yet, we recommend one based on your team size and budget, often HubSpot's free CRM, Pipedrive or a simple Airtable base.
