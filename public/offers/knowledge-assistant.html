FAQ

Common questions.

Will it make things up?

We write guardrails so it answers from your approved documents, and we test it against real questions before it goes live. When it can't answer, it says so.

Where does my data go?

For EU and UK businesses we configure consent, data retention and a clear privacy notice, and choose AI providers that offer appropriate data-processing terms. We explain the setup up front.

What documents does it need?

Whatever you already have: price lists, policies, procedures, FAQs. We help organise them, and the gaps it finds show you what to write next.
