FAQ

Common questions.

Will it make things up?

We write guardrails so it answers from your approved information, and we test it against the questions your customers really ask before it goes live.

Can I approve what it does?

Yes. You decide which actions it can take on its own and which need a person's approval first.

Which tools does it connect to?

Your calendar, CRM, email, WhatsApp and spreadsheets. We use reliable tools such as Zapier, Make, n8n and custom code via APIs where needed, and pick the simplest option that does the job.
