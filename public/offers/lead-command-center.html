FAQ

What exactly is the AI assistant?

A chat assistant on your website, and optionally WhatsApp, trained on your services, prices and FAQs. It answers questions instantly, collects the visitor's details and books calls into your calendar. It hands over to you when it isn't sure.

We write guardrails so it answers from your approved information, and we test it against the questions your customers really ask before it goes live.
