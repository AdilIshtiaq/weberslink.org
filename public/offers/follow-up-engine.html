FAQ

Common questions.

Do automated follow-ups feel spammy?

Not when they're written well. We write short, personal messages in your voice and stop the sequence as soon as the lead replies or books.

Which CRM should I use?

If you don't have one yet, we recommend one based on your team size and budget, often HubSpot's free CRM, Pipedrive or a simple Airtable base.

Can you automate WhatsApp messages?

Yes, using the WhatsApp Business Platform with approved message templates.
