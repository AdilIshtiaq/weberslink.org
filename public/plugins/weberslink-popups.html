What's inside

  • Three formats: A centred popup, a corner slide-in and an announcement bar at the top or bottom, with your headline, text, colours and button. Collect an email (with optional name and consent checkbox) or show a call-to-action button.
  • Triggers that respect visitors: Show after a delay, after scrolling a percentage, on exit intent (desktop), when a button is clicked, or straight away. Once per visit, once every N days or every time; people who subscribe are not asked again.
  • Precise targeting: Everywhere, the home page only, or a list of pages with wildcards. Never on checkout or thank-you pages. Desktop or mobile only, logged-out visitors only, and start and end dates for sales.
  • Leads you can use: Search the leads list, export CSV (spreadsheet formulas neutralised), or send each new lead as signed JSON to Zapier, Make, n8n or your CRM. Optional email notification, capped so bots cannot flood your inbox.
  • Spam protection: A hidden honeypot field, per-visitor rate limits, duplicate emails ignored and an optional consent checkbox keep your list clean.
  • Measured and accessible: Views, sign-ups and conversion rate for the last 30 days. Dialogs trap focus, close with Escape, return focus afterwards and have large touch targets.

Install in 3 steps

  1. Download the ZIP above.
  2. In WordPress go to Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin, choose the ZIP, install and activate.
  3. Open Popups → Add new, write your message, choose when and where it shows, save, then click Preview.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is Weberslink Popups & Lead Capture free?

Yes. It is a free download under the GPL and works on as many of your own WordPress sites as you like.

Can it send leads to Mailchimp, Klaviyo or my CRM?

Use the webhook with Zapier, Make or n8n, or post straight to an endpoint that accepts JSON. A signature header lets you verify each request came from your site. You can also export a CSV at any time.

Will popups hurt my SEO?

Large popups that cover the page right after arrival on mobile can. Use a delay, a slide-in or a bar, and limit full-screen popups on phones.

Is it GDPR friendly?

You can require a consent checkbox with your own wording, and the leads list lets you delete any lead. Visitors' IP addresses are not stored, only a one-way fingerprint used for rate limiting. This is not legal advice.

Does it work with caching plugins?

Yes. Page targeting is decided on the server for each URL, while triggers, devices and frequency run in the browser.

Which WordPress and PHP versions does it need?

WordPress 5.9 or newer and PHP 7.4 or newer.
