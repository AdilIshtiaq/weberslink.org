✨ Describe a campaign

Gemini sets the places and business types from your leads. You check the live count, then save.

A lead goes to the first campaign (top to bottom) that fits it. Leave a box empty to mean "any". Places come from your spreadsheet's country, state and city columns (state and city are also read from a US street address). Business types match as words inside the lead's category, niche, store name or website, so "roof" catches "Roofing contractor". Until you create a campaign every lead is emailed; after that, only leads in a campaign that is switched on.
