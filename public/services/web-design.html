A website should be your best salesperson

Most small business websites are digital brochures: they look fine, list some services and wait. Visitors land, scroll and leave — and you never know they were there. Our small business web design starts from the opposite direction: what does a visitor need to see, in what order, to trust you enough to get in touch?

That means a clear headline that says who you help and what result you deliver, proof near the top (reviews, real projects, credentials), obvious next steps on every screen, and forms that take less than a minute to complete.

What's included in every website

  • Custom design in Figma, approved by you before anything is built — no recycled templates.
  • Mobile-first build. Most service enquiries now start on a phone, so we design for thumbs first.
  • Speed: optimised images, minimal scripts and good hosting. We aim for green Core Web Vitals, which Google uses as a ranking signal.
  • Conversion elements: click-to-call, quote and booking forms, sticky calls-to-action, WhatsApp or SMS buttons.
  • On-page SEO: page titles, meta descriptions, headings, schema markup, sitemap and Google Search Console setup.
  • GDPR-ready privacy policy, consent on forms and privacy-friendly analytics for EU and UK visitors.
  • Training so your team can update text, photos and blog posts without calling us.

WordPress or custom code?

We build both. WordPress with Elementor suits businesses that want to edit every page themselves and add plugins over time. A lightweight custom build suits businesses that care most about speed, security and low maintenance. We'll recommend one on your strategy call — and explain why. How a website project works

  1. Strategy call — your goals, your customers, your competitors. You get a fixed quote.
  2. Design — homepage and key pages in Figma. You comment, we refine.
  3. Build — development, forms, integrations, SEO setup and testing on real devices.
  4. Launch — domain, hosting, redirects from your old site, analytics and Search Console.
  5. Support — 30 days of fixes on one-time websites; ongoing management on monthly plans.
