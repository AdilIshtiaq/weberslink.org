Signs it's time for a redesign

  • Visitors arrive but rarely call, book or fill in a form.
  • The site looks dated next to your competitors — or embarrassing on a phone.
  • Pages take more than a few seconds to load.
  • You can't update it yourself, or every change costs you money.
  • Your services, pricing or positioning have changed and the site hasn't.

Our redesign process: audit first

A redesign shouldn't throw away what already works. Before we design anything we review your analytics, Search Console data, top pages, keywords and conversion paths. Then we keep the content and URLs that earn traffic, fix what's holding you back and rebuild around a clear path to enquiry.

  1. Audit — traffic, rankings, speed, UX and conversion review.
  2. Plan — sitemap, page structure and a URL redirect map.
  3. Design & build — new design in Figma, then a fast, mobile-first build.
  4. Migrate — content, images, redirects and tracking.
  5. Launch & monitor — we watch rankings and fix any issues in the weeks after launch.
🛡️
Protecting your rankings: every old URL gets a 301 redirect to its new home, so the Google trust your site has built carries over.

FAQ

Common questions.

Will a redesign hurt my Google rankings?

Not if it's done properly. We map and redirect every old URL, keep content that ranks and monitor Search Console after launch.

Can you redesign my site on the same platform?

Yes. We can redesign on your existing WordPress, or move you to a faster platform if that's the better option.
