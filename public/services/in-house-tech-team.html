The problem with doing tech the usual way

Most growing businesses end up with a web designer for the site, a freelancer for automations, a separate chatbot tool and an agency for SEO. None of them talk to each other. When something breaks, everyone says it's someone else's problem, and you become the project manager. Hiring your own developers fixes that, but it takes months and costs full-time salaries before anything ships.

An in-house tech team from WebersLink sits in the middle: a dedicated team that works inside your business like employees would, without the recruiting, payroll or management.

What you get

  • A dedicated engineer who learns your services, customers and tools, and stays on your account.
  • The whole skill set under one roof: design, front-end and back-end development, AI agents, automation, CMS, SEO and hosting.
  • A shared progress board where you add requests and watch them move from "next" to "done".
  • Weekly updates in plain English: what shipped, what's next, and anything we need from you.
  • Monitoring and fixes. We look after what we build, so problems are caught before customers notice.
  • You own everything: code, accounts, content and domains are yours from day one.

What teams like yours ask us to build

  • A new website or a redesign, with a CMS your team can update
  • Customer portals, dashboards and internal tools
  • AI chat and voice agents that answer and book customers
  • Automations that move data between your CRM, email, sheets and accounting
  • Integrations with the software you already pay for
  • Ongoing improvements, fixes, SEO and speed work

How it works

  1. Free 20-minute call to understand your business and the work in front of you.
  2. A clear plan and a fixed monthly price based on how much work you have.
  3. Start within a week. Your engineer gets access, learns your tools and ships the first improvements.
  4. Scale up or down month to month as your needs change. Cancel anytime.

FAQ

Common questions.

How is this different from hiring a freelancer?

A freelancer usually covers one skill and one project. Our team covers design, development, AI and automation together, stays on your account, and takes responsibility for keeping things running.

How is it different from an agency?

Agencies tend to deliver a project and move on. We work like part of your company: same people every month, a shared board, and ongoing improvements rather than one-off deliverables.

Who owns the code and accounts?

You do. Everything is set up in your name or handed over to you, so you're never locked in.

Can we start small?

Yes. Many clients start with a few days a month for one project, then add more once they see how it works.

How do we communicate?

Through a shared board for requests, weekly written updates, and calls when they're useful. You'll have one main contact who knows your account.
