Ranking only matters if it brings enquiries

We don't chase traffic for its own sake. Our SEO for service businesses targets the searches your customers make when they're ready to hire — "kitchen remodeler near me", "emergency plumber [city]", "accounting firm for small business" — and makes sure the page they land on converts.

What our SEO covers

  • Technical SEO — site speed, Core Web Vitals, crawlability, indexing, redirects, structured data and sitemaps.
  • Local SEO — Google Business Profile optimisation, location and service-area pages, consistent business listings (NAP) and review strategy.
  • Keyword research — finding the high-intent searches your competitors are missing.
  • Service pages — one focused page per service, which consistently outperforms a single combined "services" page.
  • Content — helpful guides that answer the questions customers ask before they buy.
  • Links — earning mentions from relevant directories, partners, suppliers and local publications. No spammy link schemes.
  • Reporting — monthly rankings, traffic, enquiries and what we're doing next, in plain English.

How long does SEO take?

Technical fixes can show results in weeks. Ranking for competitive terms usually takes 3–6 months of consistent work, longer in crowded markets. Anyone promising guaranteed #1 rankings is guessing — we'll show you realistic targets on your strategy call.
