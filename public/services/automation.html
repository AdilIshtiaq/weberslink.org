The leads you're losing aren't on your website — they're in your inbox

Most small businesses don't have a lead problem; they have a follow-up problem. Enquiries sit unanswered for hours, quotes go out without a follow-up, and nobody remembers to ask for a review. Lead follow-up automation fixes the boring parts so your team can focus on conversations.

Automations we build most often

  • Instant lead response — a personalised email or SMS within seconds of every form submission.
  • Follow-up sequences — gentle reminders over days 1, 3 and 7 until the lead books or says no.
  • Quote chasers — automatic nudges after a quote is sent.
  • CRM sync — every lead lands in HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho, Airtable, Notion or Google Sheets with its source.
  • Appointment reminders by email, SMS or WhatsApp to cut no-shows.
  • Review requests after a job is completed, linking straight to your Google review page.
  • Reporting — a weekly summary of leads, sources and bookings in your inbox.

Tools we work with

We use reliable, well-supported tools — Zapier, Make, n8n, native CRM workflows and custom code via APIs where needed — and pick the simplest option that does the job. You own every account and every automation.

⚡
Start small. Most clients start with instant response + a 3-step follow-up. It takes days to set up and is usually the single biggest win.

Which CRM should I use?

Do automated follow-ups feel spammy?

Not when they're written well. We write short, personal messages in your voice and stop the sequence as soon as the lead replies or books.
