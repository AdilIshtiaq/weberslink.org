B2B buyers do their homework

Procurement teams and distributors compare suppliers on product range, quality standards, certifications and responsiveness. A manufacturing website has to make that comparison easy — and make it obvious how to request a quote.

What we build for manufacturers

  • Product catalogues organised the way buyers search: by category, application and specification.
  • Spec sheets and downloads — PDFs, CAD files, brochures — with optional lead capture.
  • Certifications and quality pages (ISO, CE, FDA and others) presented prominently.
  • RFQ forms that capture product, quantity, destination and timeline.
  • Multi-language options for international buyers.
  • Technical SEO for product and category pages, so buyers find you by part type and application.
