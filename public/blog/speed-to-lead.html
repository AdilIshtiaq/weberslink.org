Speed to lead: why replying in 5 minutes wins more clients

The first business to respond usually wins the job. Here's what the research says about lead response time — and how to reply to every enquiry within minutes, even when you're busy.

Updated October 2026 · 6 min read

Book a free strategy call → Get a free website audit

Speed to lead is the time between a prospect enquiring — a form, a call, a chat — and your business responding. It's one of the most overlooked drivers of revenue for small businesses.

What the research says

A widely cited lead response study (originally by InsideSales.com with MIT) found that contacting a lead within 5 minutes made a business dramatically more likely to reach them than waiting 30 minutes. More recent home-services research points the same way: the first contractor to respond wins a large share of jobs, yet most businesses take far longer than an hour to reply. See, for example, this summary of speed-to-lead statistics.

The exact numbers vary by study and industry, but the pattern is consistent: the faster you respond, the more leads you convert — and most competitors are slow.

Why small businesses respond slowly

  • The owner is on a job site, with a client or asleep.
  • Form submissions go to an inbox nobody checks often.
  • Nobody is clearly responsible for new enquiries.
  • Leads arrive in five places — website, phone, WhatsApp, Facebook, email.

6 ways to respond to every lead within minutes

  1. Send an instant auto-reply that's personal and useful: confirm what they asked for and offer a booking link.
  2. Add online booking so ready-to-go leads can pick a time immediately.
  3. Use an AI assistant on your website and WhatsApp to answer questions and qualify leads 24/7. (How our AI chatbots work.)
  4. Route every lead to one place — a CRM or shared inbox — with mobile notifications.
  5. Automate follow-ups for leads that don't respond: day 1, day 3, day 7. (Lead follow-up automation.)
  6. Measure it. Track average response time weekly. What gets measured gets faster.
⏱️
Quick test: fill in your own website's contact form right now. How long until you get a reply — and what does it say?

Speed alone isn't enough

A fast reply that says "Thanks, we'll be in touch" doesn't help much. The best responses move the lead forward: answer their question, ask one qualifying question, and offer a specific next step like a call time or booking link.

FAQ

Common questions.

What is a good lead response time?

Aim for under 5 minutes during business hours and an instant automated reply outside them. Many top performers respond in under a minute using automation or AI.

Does speed to lead matter for B2B as well as home services?

Yes. The research was originally done on B2B web leads, and the same principle applies to any business where prospects contact several providers.
