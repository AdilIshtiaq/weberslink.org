How much does a small business website cost in 2026?

Real price ranges for DIY, freelance and agency websites — what you get at each level, the hidden ongoing costs, and how to decide what's worth paying for.

Updated October 2026 · 7 min read

Book a free strategy call → Get a free website audit

Short answer: a small business website in 2026 typically costs anywhere from a few hundred dollars a year with a DIY builder to $3,000–$15,000 with a small agency, and $15,000+ for large custom builds. Most service businesses that want a professional, lead-generating site should budget roughly $1,500–$6,000 up front plus $50–$500 per month for hosting, maintenance and marketing.

These ranges are consistent with recent industry pricing surveys such as WebsiteSetup's 2026 cost guide. Prices vary a lot by country: agencies in the US, UK and Western Europe typically charge more than equally skilled teams elsewhere.

What actually drives the price

  • Number of pages — a 5-page site and a 30-page site with service-area pages are very different projects.
  • Custom design vs template — custom design costs more but looks unique and is built around your conversion goals.
  • Copywriting — writing persuasive, SEO-friendly content is often underestimated.
  • Functionality — booking, e-commerce, client portals, AI chat, CRM integrations.
  • SEO setup — technical SEO, schema, redirects from an old site.
  • Photography and branding — logo, brand guidelines, professional photos.

The ongoing costs nobody mentions

  • Domain: ~$10–$20/year.
  • Hosting: $5–$50/month for most small business sites.
  • Maintenance and updates: $0–$200/month depending on platform (WordPress needs regular plugin updates).
  • Email, analytics and tools: often $10–$100/month.
  • Marketing: SEO, ads and content — the part that actually brings visitors.

Cheap vs expensive: what's the real difference?

The cheapest website is the one that pays for itself. A $500 site that never generates an enquiry costs more than a $3,000 site that brings in one new client a month. When comparing quotes, ask each provider:

  1. How will this site turn visitors into enquiries?
  2. Is SEO set up properly — titles, schema, speed, Search Console?
  3. Can I edit it myself? Who owns the domain, hosting and code?
  4. What's included after launch, and what costs extra?

FAQ

Common questions.

What is a reasonable price for a small business website?

For a professionally designed, mobile-friendly site with proper SEO, $1,500–$6,000 is a reasonable range for most small service businesses, plus modest monthly hosting and maintenance.

Is it cheaper to build a website myself?

Up front, yes. But DIY sites often cost more in lost enquiries, time spent and eventual rebuilds. They're a good choice when you're testing an idea on a tiny budget.

Do I have to pay monthly for a website?

You'll always pay for hosting and a domain. Maintenance and marketing plans are optional but help the site keep performing.
