Who we are

WebersLink ("we", "us") is a technology company that builds automation, AI voice and chat agents, websites, software and CMS sites. Our engineering team is based in Lahore, Pakistan, and we work with clients in the United Kingdom, the United States and Europe. You can contact us about privacy at hassan@weberslink.org.

What we collect

  • Forms: when you request a call or a website audit, we collect the details you enter — such as your name, email, website, the service you're interested in, budget, timeline, message — plus your time zone so we can suggest suitable call times.
  • Technical data: our server records your IP address with form submissions to prevent spam and abuse.
  • Analytics: if enabled, we use privacy-friendly analytics that do not use cookies and do not track you across websites.
  • AI chat assistant: if you use the chat on our website, your messages are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate replies. We use Google's paid API service, under which Google does not use your messages to improve its products. The conversation is kept in our server's memory for up to 2 hours so the assistant can follow it. If you choose to share your contact details in the chat, we receive them along with a transcript of the conversation so we can follow up.
  • Emails from us: if we email your business, links in our emails go through a short weberslink.org address so we can see which links were clicked and when. We record the click, the link and your email address — not your IP address — and keep it with our notes on that conversation. Reply "unsubscribe" to any of our emails and we won't email you again.
  • Booking: if you book through our embedded calendar (Cal.com or Calendly), that provider processes your booking under its own privacy policy.

Why we use it

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry, prepare your audit or call, and send you a proposal if you ask for one. Our legal basis under the GDPR is your consent and our legitimate interest in replying to requests you make. We never sell your data and we don't add you to marketing lists without asking.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for up to 24 months so we have context if you get back in touch, unless you become a client (then for as long as needed for the project and legal obligations) or ask us to delete it sooner.

Cookies

Our own pages don't need cookies to work. We store a few small settings in your browser (for example, whether you closed the chat window) so the site remembers your choice; these never leave your device. Third-party services can set their own cookies, as described below.

Advertising

Some pages on this site, such as our guides and free plugin pages, may show advertising served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info (US) or youronlinechoices.eu (EU and UK).
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before any advertising cookies are used, using a consent message provided by Google. You can change your choice at any time from the "Privacy and cookie settings" link that appears with that message. Without consent, Google may show non-personalised ads, which use limited cookies for frequency capping and fraud prevention.

Who we share it with

Only with services we use to run our business — our email and hosting providers, our AI provider (Google) for the chat assistant, our booking tool if you use it, and Google AdSense where ads are shown (see Advertising above). Some of these providers may process data outside the EU/UK; where they do, they rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

If you're in the EU, UK or a US state with privacy laws (such as California), you can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your data, or object to how we use it. Email hassan@weberslink.org and we'll respond within 30 days. EU/UK residents can also complain to their local data protection authority.
